# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

Web app with desktop PWA installation on macOS and Windows.

## Users

Product designers start handoffs from Figma or upload a structured snapshot package. Product managers, developers, and QA review the same frozen version, inspect its design data, discuss changes, and use its evidence in implementation and release work.

## Product Purpose

Asbir Handoff is a standalone team app for turning design snapshots into versioned, reviewable implementation context. It keeps the captured canvas, exact available layout values, comments, visual and structured diffs, exports, AI notes, and task links together. Success means a team can move from a designer's capture to a developer or QA task without losing the source evidence.

## Positioning

A versioned handoff workspace that carries design evidence from a frozen Figma canvas or structured snapshot package into team review and implementation.

## Current source support

The Figma plugin and source-agnostic structured package upload are in scope. Direct Paper MCP capture is deferred.

## Brand Personality

Direct, collaborative, practical.

## Visual references

Use the OneQR Handoff reference for the review canvas layout, controls, interactions, and visual treatment. Use Linear as a quality bar for compact, precise UI. Preserve Asbir Handoff's dark canvas chrome and keep the captured source canvas visually faithful. OneQR is a source app example, not the shell's brand.

## Roles and publishing

Workspace Owners and Designer members can create handoffs, capture versions, edit changelogs, and publish. A workspace Owner may also act as a Product Designer. Designer members can own projects or be assigned as the PD-in-charge for specific projects without becoming workspace Owners. Product managers (Reviewer role), developers, and QA receive a stable canvas link and cannot start a handoff. PDs can also open the review canvas to inspect the published snapshot. The link shows the latest published version; draft versions stay private until an authorized PD publishes them.

## Design Principles

- Preserve each accepted handoff as an immutable version.
- Show whether a value comes from the source, a deterministic measurement, or unavailable data.
- Keep design, product, engineering, and QA review attached to the same version.
- Ground AI summaries and tasks in traceable snapshot evidence.
- Let teams inspect details quickly without obscuring the canvas.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Make the review interface keyboard operable, screen-reader friendly, and usable without relying on color alone to convey state.
